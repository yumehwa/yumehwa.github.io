BRAND FOUNDATION

Brand Identity

Brand identity system

We define your brand's distinct story and the impression it should leave, then translate them into a clear visual system. Rather than creating a logo in isolation, we design an identity that can grow consistently across packaging, print, and web.

Scope

  • Brand direction and core keywords
  • Logo and essential logo system
  • Color and typography standards
  • Key visuals and graphic tone

Deliverables

  • Original and ready-to-use logo files
  • Color and type usage guide
  • Essential identity applications
  • Concise brand guideline document
02

DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

Web Design

Digital brand experience

We shape a digital experience where visitors can naturally discover the brand's point of view and story. Instead of simply listing information, we design a clear journey from understanding the brand to exploring its work or products and making an inquiry.

Scope

  • Content structure and page flow
  • Responsive web UI design
  • Image and graphic direction
  • Interaction and motion guidelines

Deliverables

  • Page architecture and wireframes
  • Desktop, tablet, and mobile screens
  • Web-ready images and graphic assets
  • Static web build within the agreed scope
03

VISUAL COMMUNICATION

Graphic Design

Visual communication

We translate your message into a visual language for posters, content, advertising, and social media. Image and typography remain consistent across formats so every piece still feels unmistakably like the same brand.

Scope

  • Campaign and promotional visuals
  • Poster and banner design
  • Advertising and social templates
  • Brand graphic assets

Deliverables

  • Final artwork for each channel
  • Print and digital size adaptations
  • Reusable content templates
  • Sample content applications
04

PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

Package Design

Product experience

We carry the brand's impression from the first encounter through the moment the package is opened. Product features, retail context, and production methods are considered together to balance visual beauty with practical manufacturability.

Scope

  • Product labels and container graphics
  • Carton and shipping box design
  • Wrapping, belly bands, and stickers
  • Package opening experience

Deliverables

  • Print-ready packaging artwork
  • Size adaptations across products
  • Packaging mockup images
  • Production and finishing suggestions
05

PHYSICAL TOUCHPOINT

Print Design

Physical touchpoints

Printed pieces such as business cards, menus, and leaflets are tactile brand touchpoints that leave a lasting impression. We consider their context of use, information hierarchy, paper, and finishing details together.

Scope

  • Business cards, cards, and coupons
  • Menus, notices, and leaflets
  • Posters, banners, and signage graphics
  • Print specifications and finishing

Deliverables

  • Press-ready final artwork
  • Size adaptations by format
  • Paper and finishing guide
  • Print production support when agreed
06

BRAND OBJECTS

Goods Production

Brand objects

We plan small objects that keep your brand present in everyday life. Rather than simply placing a logo on a product, we propose items and sets that reflect the brand's mood and intended experience.

Scope

  • Goods selection and set planning
  • Stickers, postcards, and cards
  • Keyrings and small accessories
  • Goods packaging and inserts

Deliverables

  • Production design for each item
  • Final manufacturing files
  • Product set mockups
  • Supplier coordination when agreed
